Your First Darkroom Workshop

What to bring and what happens when the safelight comes on.

Bring developed black-and-white film negatives and choose a few photographs you’d like to turn into prints. If you’re unsure which frames to bring, we can look through them together.

Wear comfortable clothes and closed shoes. Everything else is provided, including photographic paper, chemicals and equipment.

We’ll begin by introducing you to the darkroom and the tools we’ll be using. You’ll see how an enlarger projects the image from your negative onto photographic paper, how a timer controls the exposure, and how the paper is developed in a series of chemical baths until the photograph slowly appears.

Then it’s your turn. You’ll make test strips, adjust the exposure, and work towards making your own photographic print.

No previous darkroom experience is needed. We’ll take things step by step, so you can understand the process as you go and leave with a photograph you’ve made yourself.

If you’d like to try it yourself, book a darkroom workshop.
