  • Bring your exposed film to us in Moutoullas. Label each roll with your name.
  • We develop by hand with great care, but film can be affected by things outside our control, such as how it was stored, shot or loaded. If a roll is damaged while we develop it, we refund the developing fee for that roll.
  • Collect your film within 60 days. After that, we may dispose of it.
