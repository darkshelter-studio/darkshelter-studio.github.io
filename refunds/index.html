Refunds & cancellations

Plain rules for when plans change. If anything here is unclear, contact us at info@darkshelter.studio or +357 96 530552 before you book.

If you cancel a workshop

  • Cancel 7 days or more before the workshop: full refund.
  • Cancel less than 7 days before: no refund.
  • If you don’t come on the day: no refund.

To cancel, email or call us. The 7 days count from when we receive your message.

If you cancel a camera rental

  • Cancel 7 days or more before your pickup date: full refund.
  • Cancel less than 7 days before, or don’t collect the camera: no refund.

If we cancel

If we have to cancel a workshop or can’t provide a camera you booked, you choose: a full refund, or a new date at no extra cost.

Film developing

We take great care with every roll. If a roll is damaged while we develop it, we refund the developing fee for that roll. We can’t pay for the value of the photographs, the film itself, or any other costs.

Camera rentals: damage, loss and late return

  • Late return: the daily rental price is charged for each extra day.
  • Damage: you pay the cost of repair.
  • Loss, theft, or damage beyond repair: you pay the cost of replacing the camera with an equivalent used one.

If a camera stops working during your rental through no fault of yours, tell us and we’ll replace it or refund the unused days.

How refunds are paid

Refunds go back to the card you paid with, usually within 5–10 working days depending on your bank. If you paid in person, we refund the same way where possible.

Your 14-day right to cancel

Under EU consumer law, workshops and camera rentals booked for a specific date are leisure services, so the general 14-day right to cancel online purchases does not apply. The rules on this page apply instead. Nothing here affects your other legal rights as a consumer.
