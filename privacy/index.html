Privacy policy

This page explains what personal details we collect when you book, pay or contact us, what we use them for, and your rights. We keep it to the minimum we need.

Who is responsible

Darkshelter is run by Grigoris Gregoriou, sole trader, Moutoullas, Cyprus. He is responsible for your personal details. Contact: info@darkshelter.studio or +357 96 530552.

What we collect

  • Contact details: your name, email address and phone number.
  • Booking details: what you booked, the date, the number of people, and anything you tell us, such as your film experience or allergies relevant to the darkroom.
  • Camera rentals: a note that we checked your photo ID when you collected the camera. We don’t keep a copy of your ID.
  • Payments: amount, date and payment status. Card details are handled by Stripe or Revolut, not by us. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Messages: emails and messages you send us.

Why we use it

  • To manage your booking, rental or film developing, and contact you about it.
  • To take payment and give refunds.
  • To keep the accounting and tax records Cyprus law requires.
  • To answer your questions.

We use your details because we need them to provide what you booked, and because the law requires us to keep business records. We don’t send marketing emails unless you ask us to, and you can stop them at any time.

Who we share it with

We never sell your details. We share them only with services that help us run the business, and only what they need:

  • Stripe and Revolut, to process card payments and refunds.
  • Our email provider, to send and receive emails.
  • Our accountant and the Cyprus tax authorities, where the law requires it.

Some of these providers may process data outside the EU. When they do, they use the safeguards required by EU data protection law.

How long we keep it

We keep booking and contact details for as long as we need them to provide the service and deal with any questions afterwards. Payment and accounting records are kept for as long as Cyprus tax law requires. After that, we delete them.

This website

This website does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It loads fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google’s servers and shares your IP address with them. When you pay online, Stripe may use cookies to keep payments secure and prevent fraud.

Your rights

You can ask us to see the personal details we hold about you, correct them, delete them, or stop using them, and you can ask for a copy to take elsewhere. Some records we must keep by law. Email us and we’ll reply within one month.

If you’re unhappy with how we handle your details, you can complain to the Office of the Commissioner for Personal Data Protection in Cyprus.
